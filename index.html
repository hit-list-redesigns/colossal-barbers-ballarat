Train. Then trim.

Colossal is the barbershop inside D2E Gym at Raydon Court in Delacombe. It's Ballarat's first in-gym barbershop, so your fade is a few steps from the squat rack.

It's a small, owner-run shop. Maddie owns it, Sophia works the second chair, and the shop has been cutting the west side of Ballarat for six years.

  • Inside D2E Gym, 5/6 Raydon Court, Delacombe
  • Fades, buzz cuts, beard work and cut throat shaves. Full list above.
  • Beard oils on the shelf from Jericho Australia and Beard On

First cuts to fresh fades
